Chicken Stir Fry

Description

Stir Fry dates back to antient China as an economical way to cook with less fuel due to the scarcity of wood and charcoal. The technique involved quickly cooking bite sized pieces of meat, vegetables and grains in a wok. As Chinese immigrants traveled the globe they brought with them their traditin of stir fry

This recipe is loaded with with fresh veggies and a delicious sauce. It's healthy and can be made in about 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken cut in to 1 inch cubes
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cups of broccoli florets
  • 3 carrots sliced
  • 1 bell pepper sliced
  • 2 tsp minced ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves minced

Stir Fry Sauce

  • 1 Tbsp corn starch
  • 2 Tbsp cold water
  • 1/4 cup chicken broth
  • 3 Tbsp low sodium soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes

Instructions

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together corn starch at water. Add remaining sauce ingredients (chicken broth, soy sauce, sesame oil, red pepper flakes) and whisk to combine. Set aside.
  2. Add one tablespoon of olive oil to a large skillet or wok and heat over medium high heat.
  3. Add chicken (in batches if necessary) and season with salt and pepper. Cook for 3-5 minutes or until cooked through. Remove from skillet.
  4. Reduce heat to medium and add remaining oil to skillet
  5. Add broccoli, bell pepper and carrots and cook stirring occasionally, just until crisp tender. Add ginger and garlic and cook for an additional minute.
  6. Add chicken back into the skillet and stir to combine.
  7. Whisk stir fry sauce and pour over chicken and vegetables and stir gently to combine.
  8. Bring to boil, stirring occasionally. Let boil for one minute.
  9. Serve with white rice if desired.
