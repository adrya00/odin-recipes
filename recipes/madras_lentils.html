Madras Lentils

Description

Madras lentils is a westernized term for a dish based on tradtitional India lentil dishes like Dal Makhani which uses lentils and kidney beans

In this version I forgo the kidney beans and add potatos instead. It is super easy to make and extremely delicious. This is the instant pot version of the dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of dried lentils
  • 1 15oz can of tomato sauce
  • 1/2 onion finely diced
  • 1 large russet potato peeled and cubed
  • 1 15oz can of unsweetened coconut milk
  • 1 cup of vetable broth
  • 3 Tbsp of butter
  • 3 gloves of garlic minced
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • crushed red pepper flakes to taste
  • 1 tsp of thai red curry paste (optional)

Instructions

  1. Put instant pot on saute and add butter. Add lentils and saute for about 3-5 mins, then turn off saute function
  2. Dump evertything else into the instant pot and give it a stir
  3. Set instant pot for high pressure 15 minutes, then allow it a 5 min natural release.
  4. Serve with white rice or quinoa

Tip

When I first started making this I would get a burn notice on the instapot often. This is why I added sauting the lentils in the butter to help reduce this. It took a few tries for me to get the timing right but I don't get the notice anymore.
