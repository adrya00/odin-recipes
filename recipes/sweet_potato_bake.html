Sausage and Sweet Potato Bake

I discovered this recipe a while ago. I had never really been a fan of sweet potatoes but this changed my mind. Really easy to make. You can use whatever type of sausage you want. Unfortuently I don't have an image for this yet.

Ingredients

  • 3 large sweet potatoes
  • 1 lb of sausage (any sausage is fine but I use the large hot sausge links that come in a pack of 5)
  • 1/2 an onion
  • 1 apple
  • olive oil
  • cinnamon
  • garlic powder
  • chili powder
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat oven to 375F degrees
  2. Peel sweet potatoes and chop onto 1 inch chunks. Peel apple and cut both apple and onion into bite sized chunks
  3. Put sweet potatoes, apples and onions into 13x9 baking dish. Drizzle with olive oil and make sure everything is coated.
  4. I mix all the spices in a small bowl then sprinkle over veggies. Toss to make sure everything is coated in the spices as well.
  5. For the type of sasuage I use, I remove outer casing and pull off 1 inch pieces and form into meatballs. Then I place the meatballs throughout the baking dish. I leave them on top so their flavor will run down.
  6. Bake uncovered for about 1 to 1 1/2 hours until the sausage is browned and potatoes are tender.
